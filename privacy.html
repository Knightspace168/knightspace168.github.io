Privacy policy

Hermes · last updated 9 November 2026

Who uses Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What Google data it uses

Hermes asks for access to the owner's Google Calendar. It uses that access only to read the owner's events and to create or change events the owner asks for, such as work shifts and bill reminders.

Where the data goes

  • Sign-in tokens and calendar data stay on the owner's own computer.
  • Calendar details may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Hermes's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token file.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Hermes.
